Research Agenda

Research

My research explores how deep learning, remote sensing, and agricultural modeling can support smart agriculture, climate adaptation, and sustainable farming practices.

Smart Agriculture and Agricultural Modeling

Applying machine learning and process-based modeling to crop production, field management, yield prediction, and agricultural decision support.

Deep Learning and Computer Vision

Developing deep learning and computer vision workflows for crop monitoring, disease assessment, phenology, and other agricultural observations.

Remote Sensing and Climate Change

Combining remote sensing, environmental data, and climate information to understand agricultural responses and improve adaptation strategies.
