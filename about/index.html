Biography

Academic Background

I am a PhD candidate at Northwest A&F University with a strong focus on deep learning applications in agriculture. My research explores the intersection of advanced computational methods and ecological/agricultural modeling to support sustainable farming practices.

I welcome collaborations on smart agriculture, climate change, remote sensing, machine learning, computer vision, and agricultural modeling.
